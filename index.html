How to defend

Build towers on the glowing pads: pick one from the bar (or press 1–6), then click a pad. Click a tower to upgrade or sell it.

Upgrade paths. Every tower has three paths, but you can only invest in two, and only one of those can ascend to a fourth tier. Choose what this spot needs. Each tower can also pick its target: first, last, strongest or closest.

Heroes. Move the Aether Warden with WASD, or click the ground to send the selected hero. Warden: Q dash, E starfall. The Thornkeeper joins later: R roots, F bulwark. Tab switches heroes.

Warden powers charge as shadows fall: Z X C V. Traps go on the paths and refresh every wave.

On a phone: tap a tower, then a glowing pad. Tap the ground to move your hero. Powers and traps live on the right edge; tap one, then tap where it should go.

Begin wave when you are ready (Space). Calling early pays a little bonus. Hover any shadow to learn its weakness.
